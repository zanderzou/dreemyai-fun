**Dreemy's public pages do not give one consistent description of renewal.** Its [terms](https://www.dreemy.ai/terms) describe subscription cancellation to prevent future charges. Its [refund and payment page](https://www.dreemy.ai/refund-policy) says there are no recurring subscriptions and describes credit-package purchases instead.

We checked both pages on October 19, 2026. We did not purchase a plan, inspect an authenticated billing screen or resolve the conflict with support.

## A public description is not your receipt

Possible explanations include an outdated page or different purchase types. The pages alone do not establish which explanation applies. Do not select the more convenient wording and present it as a confirmed account state.

Start with the accepted purchase terms, product name, amount, currency, billing frequency and any next charge date shown. Preserve the purchase date because later public wording may not describe an earlier transaction accurately.

## Read the renewal state directly

If an account lists a future recurring payment, preserve that observation and the subscription reference. If it lists only a completed credit-package purchase, record that instead. A generic plan badge or a current balance does not settle the billing frequency by itself.

For an illustrative example, a receipt for one completed 50-unit package does not prove there is a subscription. Conversely, a public sentence saying there is no renewal cannot explain away an account record that actually shows a next recurring charge. Either mismatch deserves a specific explanation.

## Refund and renewal are separate decisions

The terms describe a restrictive refund position with a mandatory-law qualification. The dedicated payment page describes a limited refund window and usage conditions. Those statements should not be converted into a guaranteed approval or a universal rejection by this guide.

Keep a repayment inquiry tied to its purchase, actual usage and applicable conditions. Stopping a future charge and repaying an earlier charge answer different questions.

Do not make a second purchase to test which policy is applied. That adds another transaction without resolving the first.

## Ask which conditions govern the identified purchase

The [official contact page](https://www.dreemy.ai/contact-us) asks for a clear issue description and relevant account/device evidence. A useful billing inquiry identifies the receipt, describes the observed renewal state and links the conflicting public pages.

Ask the provider to confirm the purchase type, whether any renewal is active and which refund conditions it applies to that transaction. Keep credentials and full payment-card details out of the request.

Until that question is resolved, the defensible statement is that the published descriptions conflict. An accurate article should preserve that uncertainty rather than claiming all Dreemy customers either always renew or never renew.
