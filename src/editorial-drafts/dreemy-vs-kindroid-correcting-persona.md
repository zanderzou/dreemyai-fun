Dreemy AI vs Kindroid can be evaluated through a small revision rather than a long list of personality labels. If a character's hometown is wrong, the useful question is whether you can correct that fact while preserving its voice, role and current story.

This article reviews official information on October 8, 2026. The original exercise uses adult fictional characters and does not report tested compliance or measured recall.

## Create a fact with clear neighbors

Use a fictional archivist named Rowan. Rowan lives in Harborwick, speaks in short practical sentences and is helping organize an exhibition of invented maps. Suppose the conversation starts placing Rowan in Pineford instead.

The intended correction is the hometown only. The archivist's occupation, concise style and exhibition task should remain unchanged. Keep a copy of this small specification outside the service so you can distinguish a successful correction from an attractive but different persona.

## Check whose definition you control

On Dreemy, inspect the current creation or editing route for your own character. If you selected somebody else's public character, distinguish a request made in chat from a supported edit to its definition. Those actions may influence the conversation differently.

Kindroid documents editable personality controls. Identify the field relevant to a stable fictional fact and change only that fact. Copy the existing text first; replacing a whole profile with a new generated draft introduces additional changes that this exercise cannot isolate.

| Item | Intended result after correction |
|---|---|
| Hometown | Harborwick |
| Occupation | Archivist |
| Dialogue style | Short, practical sentences |
| Current task | Preparing the map exhibition |
| Player decisions | Remain with the player |

## Use an indirect follow-up

After the correction, ask Rowan to suggest where the exhibition notice should be posted locally. Check whether the answer uses the intended hometown naturally. Avoid asking “Do you remember Harborwick?” as the only follow-up because that supplies the corrected name in the question.

Next, ask about the exhibition's remaining task. Look for unintended changes such as a new profession, a much more elaborate voice or a fabricated agreement. A reply can contain the right hometown while still drifting away from the persona you intended to maintain.

## Log the mechanism and the result separately

Record whether you edited a persistent field, corrected a message or supplied a new scene note. Also record whether the next replies followed it. The presence of a control and the quality of a particular response are separate observations.

If the correction fails, restate it once clearly and inspect the relevant setup rather than adding contradictory notes throughout the chat. Keep the exercise small enough to repeat after a later update.
