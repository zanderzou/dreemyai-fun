Give one backstory detail a small action in the present scene. A long biography can contain many facts without showing the reader how the character behaves.

Dreemy's FAQ describes custom bots with personality, backstory and appearance. The exercise below is a writing method for those available description fields, not a claim that a product stores every detail perfectly.

## Choose a habit that reveals attention

For an original adult character, imagine Mara, a 44-year-old botanical illustrator. Her useful habit is checking one leaf closely before making a field note.

That action can reveal patience and interest without a paragraph announcing that she is thoughtful, observant and passionate.

## Connect the habit to a reason

A compact backstory note could be: “Mara illustrates plants for a small local collection. Before sketching, she compares the leaf edge and vein pattern. She asks what the other person notices before giving her own view.”

The occupation explains the behavior. The final sentence gives the reader a natural opportunity to participate.

Avoid making the habit compulsory in every reply. If Mara checks a leaf during a train delay, a formal dinner and an urgent interruption, the detail has become a repetitive cue rather than a believable preference.

## Leave an observable opening

A scene could begin with Mara placing a leaf beside the closed notebook and asking, “Does this edge look smooth or finely toothed to you?” The player can answer, ask a question or take a different action.

Do not write the player's answer into her description. Keep the character's habit distinct from a guaranteed response by someone else.

## Review behavior, then refine the brief

Read a relevant reply for the intended behavior: careful observation, a specific question and room for participation. If the detail becomes a repeated slogan, shorten or reframe the description where current editing controls permit it.

Use original fictional adults and respect Dreemy's eligibility and content rules. This example does not upload a real person's image or claim a hands-on bot result. A useful habit earns its place by creating a small scene action the reader can understand.
