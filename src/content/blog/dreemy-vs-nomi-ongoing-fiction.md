Dreemy AI vs Nomi for ongoing fiction is less about the first greeting than the second visit. A compelling opening is useful, but a serialized story also needs a recognizable character, stable world facts and a clear way to resume the same thread.

Official information was reviewed on October 8, 2026. This guide proposes a non-explicit story exercise with original adult fictional characters. It reports no account-level memory benchmark and does not treat provider marketing as an independently measured result.

## Define what must survive the break

Imagine a lighthouse keeper and a visiting cartographer discussing a missing coastal chart. The keeper has agreed to check a locked cabinet tomorrow. The cartographer has not yet chosen whether to stay overnight. Those two facts play different roles: one is established canon, and the other is an open player decision.

Write a short return note containing the character names, the agreed task and the unresolved choice. Keep it free of real personal information. This note supplies an external reference against which you can judge the next session.

## Compare discovery with maintained context

Dreemy's public site describes character browsing and chat, including different account routes. Inspect how the particular conversation is saved and reopened. Starting without an account should not be interpreted as a promise of permanent history or proof that no data is processed.

Nomi documents Backstory+ for important character and world context. Use supported persistent fields for stable facts and keep the immediate scene concise. That context can help establish the intended story; it does not guarantee that every returning detail will be correct.

| Return check | Acceptable evidence |
|---|---|
| Same character | The intended character and thread are open |
| Established task | The cabinet check is still part of the scene |
| Open decision | The visitor's overnight choice remains undecided |
| Narrow correction | A wrong detail can be corrected without a new plot |
| Usable restart | You can resume with a manageable recap if needed |

## Resume without feeding the answer first

Ask, “What did we agree to do next?” before pasting the return note. Record whether the reply recalls the cabinet task, invents a different agreement or asks for clarification. A clarification is preferable to confidently deciding that the visitor already stayed overnight.

Then provide the short note and continue for two turns. Judge the corrected continuation separately from the unsupported initial recall. If you supply the whole answer first, you are assessing how well it follows a recap, not what it retained on its own.

## Choose the routine you can keep

Consider a discovery-led route if you enjoy trying different fictional premises. Consider a context-maintenance route if you want to develop one relationship over time. Check supported creation, saving and recovery controls rather than assuming the services fit only one category.
