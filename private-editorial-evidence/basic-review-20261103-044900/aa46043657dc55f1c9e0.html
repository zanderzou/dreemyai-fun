Privacy Policy

At Dreemy AI, protecting your privacy is fundamental to our mission of enabling unrestricted creativity. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our AI chat, image, and video generation platform.

1. Information Collection

We believe in data minimization. We only collect what is necessary to provide our services:

1.1 Account Data

When you register, we collect your email address and encrypted password. We do not require real-name verification.

1.2 AI Interaction Data

This includes the "Inputs" (text prompts, uploaded reference images) you provide and the "Outputs" (content generated by the AI). We process this data to deliver the service and improve model performance.

1.3 Usage & Technical Data

We automatically log IP addresses, browser types, and device information. This helps us prevent fraud, manage GPU load balancing, and ensure the security of our infrastructure.

2. Use of Information

Your data is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To generate and render the content you request.
  • Platform Security: To detect and prevent abuse, botting, and illegal activities.
  • Model Improvement: Anonymized interaction data helps us refine the quality and safety of our AI models.
  • Communication: To send critical account notifications or security alerts.

3. Content Safety & Prohibited Use

We prioritize the safety and privacy of individuals. While using Dreemy AI, the following data practices are strictly prohibited and actively monitored:

  • Harm to Others & Harassment: You must not generate content intended to defame, mock, bully, discriminate against, or cause psychological, emotional, or reputational harm to any individual or group.
  • No Deepfakes: You must not generate or upload realistic depictions of real individuals without their explicit consent.
  • No Harmful Content: We strictly prohibit content that promotes self-harm, extreme violence, terrorism, or illegal acts.
  • Privacy Violations: Do not input personally identifiable information (PII) such as home addresses, phone numbers, or social security numbers into public chat bots or prompts.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share data only when necessary:

  • Service Providers: We utilize trusted third-party vendors for cloud hosting and payment processing. They are contractually bound to protect your data.
  • Legal Obligations: We may disclose information if required by law, such as in response to a valid court order or to protect the safety and rights of any person.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account remains active. You may request account deletion at any time. Upon deletion, your personal identifiers are removed from our active databases immediately.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. If you wish to exercise these rights, please contact our support team directly.

7. Children's Privacy

Dreemy AI is intended solely for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. Any account found to belong to a minor will be terminated immediately.

8. Geographic Restrictions (China & Hong Kong)

Warning: Dreemy AI's services are restricted and not intended for use by individuals located in, residing in, or citizens of Mainland China and the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (SAR). We do not intentionally collect or process personal data from users in these restricted regions. If we discover that a user from these regions has registered an account or provided personal data, we will immediately delete their data, terminate the account without refund, and block future access in accordance with our Terms of Service.
